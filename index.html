Who we are

Our Mission

Grassroots talent often goes unnoticed. We change that.

Benefactor Foundation is a non-profit talent empowerment organisation dedicated to discovering raw, grassroots ability and turning it into real opportunity. We believe talent is evenly distributed, but opportunity is not — and our job is to level the playing field.

Through interactive sponsorship, mentorship and structured training, we fund the dreams of young athletes, artists, builders, technologists and entrepreneurs from the ground up — providing the tools, platforms and networks they need to succeed.

What we support

Program Categories

Five pillars of grassroots talent we nurture every single year.

⚽

Sports

Football, athletics, boxing, basketball & more. Equipment, coaching, nutrition and competition exposure.

  • Training equipment & kits
  • Certified coaches & fitness plans
  • Nutrition & medical support
  • Tournament & trial placement
🎨

Arts

Music, painting, dance, drama and writing — materials, studio time, exhibition and performance stages.

  • Materials, instruments & studio time
  • Artist & industry mentorships
  • Exhibitions & stage showcases
  • Portfolio & audition preparation
💻

Technology

Coding bootcamps, robotics, digital skills, devices and mentorship from industry professionals.

  • Laptops, devices & data bundles
  • Full-stack & robotics bootcamps
  • 1-on-1 industry mentorship
  • Internship & project placement
📈

Entrepreneurship

Seed funding, business mentorship and market access for young founders and small traders.

  • Seed grants & micro-loans
  • Business planning workshops
  • Mentor pairing with founders
  • Marketplace & vendor access
🛠️

Physical Works

Vocational trades — carpentry, masonry, welding, tailoring — with tools and apprenticeship support.

  • Professional toolkits & safety gear
  • Certified apprenticeship placements
  • Trade certifications
  • First-contract job placement
